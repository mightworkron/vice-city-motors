## Structural note (permanent — mixed execution, see DT-006)

TSM operates its own collision repair operation and performs this work directly. Some
specific jobs are routed to licensed third-party facilities.

This means:

- The line is direct. First person plural is correct.
- TSM must **never** claim that all collision work is performed in-house.
- TSM must **never** describe a specific case as performed in-house unless the user has
  confirmed that execution as an input for that post.

When execution for a referenced case is third-party or mixed, write about what TSM owns:
the intake, the damage assessment, the repair plan, the standards required, the
supervision, the quality control, and the result delivered to the customer.

## Generate the post

Follow the Project Instructions. Apply everything specific to `collision_repair`:

1. Direct voice — first person plural. Respect the mixed-execution note above: never
   claim all work is in-house, never attribute execution of a specific case without
   confirmation.
2. The public SEO name is ALWAYS **"Luxury Collision Repair"**. Never "Collision &
   Repair", never any "Insurance" variant, never another public-facing line name.
3. Audience segmentation is mandatory. The post must clearly address either
   high-end/exotic owners OR mid-range/standard owners. Do not write one
   undifferentiated post for both.
4. If the post discusses TSM's service, work performed, a client case, or a technical
   recommendation, a verifiable TSM-specific element is mandatory. If none is available,
   stop or redirect to an informational / 30% bucket post.
5. **Insurance is not a prohibited keyword.** Insurance-related terms may be used when
   they reflect legitimate collision-repair search intent. Their use does NOT authorize
   copy that presents TSM as managing, negotiating, adjusting, settling, or representing
   an insurance claim.
6. Never promise or imply that TSM accepts ALL insurance carriers.
7. Never claim exceptional or exclusive ability to negotiate with, represent the client
   before, or obtain outcomes from an insurer.
8. When relevant:
   - Florida §626.9743 may be cited for anti-steering / consumer choice context.
   - Florida §501.33 may be cited when aftermarket crash parts are discussed.
   - Florida §626.854 is a risk boundary only and must NOT be cited in customer-facing copy.
9. Any proprietary metric (rating, review count, customer count) must be verified for
   source and date before use, and stated with its date. Do not use a figure the user
   has not confirmed for this post.
10. Do not fabricate repair cases, insurer relationships, technical findings, photos,
    turnaround times, or outcomes.
11. Technical detail is welcome when grounded in a real case or verified source: repair
    planning, structural work, refinishing, calibration, parts decisions, fit/finish, and
    quality-control considerations.

Deliver in the standard output format including the self-verification checklist.
